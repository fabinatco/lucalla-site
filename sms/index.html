Where things stand

Lucalla can record optional consent for future marketing texts. Sender verification, carrier registration and tested STOP/HELP handling must be completed before any message is sent.

Proposed program

Recurring automated marketing texts from Lucalla, operated by FN Developments LLC, about its reception software, demo previews, product updates and offers. No patient or medical content. Lucalla does not send texts for client clinics or on their behalf.

Separate, optional consent

The demo-inquiry form has an unchecked checkbox for marketing texts. Sending an inquiry, or giving a phone number, without checking it does not record consent. Consent is not a condition of purchase or of a demo.

Consent disclosure shown on the form

"I agree to receive recurring automated marketing text messages from Lucalla, operated by FN Developments LLC, about its reception software, demo previews, product updates and offers at the number I provide. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help when messaging begins. Consent is optional and is not a condition of purchase."

Opt-out and help

Once messaging begins, reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Until then, to withdraw a recorded consent choice, email hello@lucalla.com.
