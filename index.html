FAQ

Questions

What happens after the 7 days?

The browser demo is not a paid trial and does not start a subscription. The published plan amounts are unchanged, but production activation and billing require separate confirmation.

Do I need to change my phone number?

The browser demo needs no phone forwarding. A real phone setup has not been certified for your business. Do not forward customer calls before setup and testing.

Does it connect to my calendar?

Not in the public demo. The workspace has calendar setup and rule-review flows, but automatic real-use booking is not active by default. Any booking needs the required permissions and caller confirmation.

Does it replace my CRM?

No CRM connection is available in the current workspace. Pipedrive, Attio and HubSpot are not verified one-click production integrations. Customer memory is not active.

Is it HIPAA compliant?

No. Do not use the demo with patient health information. Use fictional details only. The browser demo does not create appointments, send messages or pass on callback requests.
