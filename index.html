FAQ

Questions

What happens after the 7 days?

The trial starts with a card on file and costs nothing for 7 days. After that we charge the plan you picked, unless you cancel before the 7 days end.

What counts as a call minute?

The time Lucalla is on a call with your caller, rounded up to the minute.

Do I need to change my phone number?

No. You keep your number and forward calls to Lucalla when you want it to answer.

Is it HIPAA compliant?

Not yet. We do not handle patient records today, and the trial should not be used with patient health information. Lucalla only answers general questions and takes appointment requests.

Does it connect to my calendar?

Not yet. Lucalla does not book into your calendar today. During the trial we agree with you how appointment requests reach your team. Calendar connection is planned.
