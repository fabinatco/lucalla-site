FAQ

Questions

What happens after the 7 days?

The trial costs nothing and no card is taken today - billing is not live yet. When it goes live, you pick a plan and only then is anything charged. We will never charge you without telling you first.

Do I need to change my phone number?

No. You keep your number and forward calls to Lucalla when you want it to answer.

Does it connect to my calendar?

Yes. Lucalla checks real availability and books straight into your calendar, so there are no double bookings.

Does it replace my CRM?

No, it feeds it. Lucalla connects to Pipedrive, Attio or HubSpot in one click, and every customer gets a memory card with summaries of every call and chat.

Is it HIPAA compliant?

Not yet. We do not handle patient records today, and the trial should not be used with patient health information. Lucalla only answers general questions and takes appointment requests.
