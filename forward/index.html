Before forwarding real calls

The public Lucalla demo runs in your browser. It does not give you a production phone number or activate call forwarding.

Do not forward customer calls to the demo

A working browser conversation is not proof that a business phone line, calendar booking or owner notifications are ready.

What a real setup needs

  1. A dedicated number assigned and confirmed for your business, with provider costs and terms approved.
  2. Carrier-specific instructions for the exact forwarding mode you want, and a tested way to switch it off.
  3. Reviewed business facts, permissions, privacy rules and any required recording notices.
  4. An end-to-end test of answering, any permitted calendar actions and any notifications, including failure handling.

Forwarding does not cancel itself

Do not assume a trial ending disables forwarding at your carrier. Switch it off through your carrier and verify calls reach your usual line. Confirm the current instructions directly with your carrier before changing settings.

No forwarding codes or universal activation timeline are promised here. Questions: hello@lucalla.com.
