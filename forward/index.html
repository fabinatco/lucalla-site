Forward your calls to Lucalla

Your phone keeps its number. When you cannot pick up, the call rings through to your Lucalla number and the receptionist answers from your website. Nothing to install and no equipment.

1. Get your Lucalla number

We send it to you by email when your trial starts. Below, 5551234567 stands for that number. Use your own 10 digits.

2. Forward only the calls you miss

Your carrierTurn on (unanswered calls)Turn off
Verizon (mobile)Dial *71 then the number, for example *715551234567, then press callDial *73
T-Mobile (mobile)Dial **61*15551234567# then press call. For busy calls too, also dial **67*15551234567#Dial ##61# (and ##67#), or ##004# to clear everything
iPhone, any carrierSettings, Phone, Call Forwarding. This forwards every call, so use the carrier code above to forward only missed callsSame screen, switch off
Office phone system, landline, AT&T or another carrierLook for "forward when no answer" or "forward when busy" in your phone settings or provider account. Email hello@lucalla.com and we will send the exact steps for your setupSame setting, switch off

Codes come from Verizon's and T-Mobile's own support pages. Carriers can change them. If a code does not work, tell us the carrier and we will find the right one.

3. Test it

Ask a friend to call your number and let it ring. After a few rings the Lucalla receptionist should answer with your business name.

Turn it off any time

Use the "Turn off" code in the table, or switch forwarding off in your phone settings. The trial also stops by itself after 7 days, and your number goes back to normal.
