    XLSX/PPTX may include hidden content. XLSX values are raw and formula results may be old. Review before saving.

    What is saved?

    Limits: 20 PDF pages, 10 XLSX sheets / 2,000 cells, 50 PPTX slide parts and 48,000 text characters. Imports are unreviewed and not saved until you save the facts. PDF and Office text is read in your browser; original files are not uploaded. XLSX uses raw stored cell values, not displayed date/currency formatting. Formula results may be stale and are labeled, never recalculated. XLSX/PPTX use internal part labels, which may differ from sheet names and slide order. Office/PDF imports do not include images, charts, hidden-content filtering, speaker notes or formatting. Separate image OCR is available for PNG/JPEG; No audio transcription or general video understanding. Sampled-frame video text is available separately within the stated limits. Check for confidential content before saving. DOCX excludes headers, footnotes and comments.
