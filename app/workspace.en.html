The number is not connected. Purchases require approval of prices and terms. Existing-number eligibility checks will cover manual app use and effects on linked devices.

Customers and shared memory

Customer memory is not active. Shared customer records across channels require verified identity and separation between businesses. Names support lookup, not authentication. Customer linking will require verification; SMS codes are an option that is not active yet.

CRM and service systems will be selected per business. HubSpot has not been selected and no account has been created.

Recordings and transcripts

Requirement: all calls, with account-based access permissions. A recording announcement will play. Activation awaits the required policies and disclosures.

The limit is three calendar months. Selecting 90 days will not exceed that limit.

Original requirement: warn one month and one week before deletion so files can be downloaded, then delete. Short-retention options are proposals, not an approved default.

Recording, storage, downloads, warnings and automatic deletion are not active.
